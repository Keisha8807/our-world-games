# Roadmap — Build the Block One Building at a Time

## Sequence (locked)

1. **#001 Mystery Dash: Rec Center Mystery** — suspense/deduction/replay
2. **#002 Style Rush** — identity/customization/collecting
3. **#003 Pet ER: Rescue Vets** — care/nurture/collecting
4. **#004 Double Dutch Hero** — rhythm/arcade/score-chase
5. **#005 Grow Your Block** — progression/economy/retention
6. **#006 Challenge Party** — elimination pressure, kid-safe mini-games
7. **Our World** — connected neighborhood (only after 3–6 proven MVPs)

## What each MVP proves

| Game | Question it answers | Kill signal |
|------|---------------------|-------------|
| Mystery Dash | Do kids replay to be the guesser / chase the Sneak? | <3 unprompted replays in 10 min |
| Style Rush | Do kids care about styling Black characters? | No replay after first theme |
| Pet ER | Do kids nurture + collect? | Animals ignored, only minigames played |
| Double Dutch | Do they chase combos/high score? | No "one more try" after fail |
| Grow Your Block | Do they return tomorrow? | Zero next-day opens |
| Challenge Party | Do they like elimination without gore? | Elimination feels bad, not funny |

## How games become the neighborhood

| Game MVP | Becomes location in Our World |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Mystery Dash | 🏀 Rec Center |
| Style Rush | 💇🏾 Style Spot (salon + barbershop + boutique) |
| Pet ER | 🐶 Pet Clinic |
| Double Dutch | 🛝 Park / Playground |
| Grow Your Block | 🌱 Community Garden |
| Challenge Party | 🎡 Arcade / Party Hall |

## Shared collection (the glue — design early, build late)

Each game feeds ONE collection book. Do NOT build cross-game saves in V1 MVPs — just keep the *idea* in mind so award names stay consistent:

- Mystery Dash → Detective Sticker Board
- Style Rush → Closet (hair, outfits, sneakers)
- Pet ER → Pet Journal
- Double Dutch → Crew Poster (moves)
- Grow Your Block → Seed Album
- Challenge Party → Trophy Wall

## V1 boundaries (applies to every MVP)

No multiplayer, accounts, chat, trading, purchases, open world, dialogue systems, or inventory economies. Local browser saves only.
