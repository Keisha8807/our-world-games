# 1. Shared cast art direction — recommended V1 test

## Direction A — warm stylized 2.5D / dimensional cartoon

Use a polished original children's-game style with:

- visibly child-aged proportions for ages 9–11
- slightly enlarged heads/eyes for gameplay readability, but **not chibi/toddler proportions**
- soft dimensional/cel-like shading that could translate into 2D sprites, layered 2.5D, or later 3D
- distinct noses, lips, eye shapes, face shapes, brows, cheeks, and skin undertones
- contemporary 2026 kid clothing silhouettes: relaxed fits, wider-leg bottoms where character-appropriate, graphic tees, sporty layers, coordinated sets, statement sneakers
- expressive hands/posture without adult fashion poses
- real hair construction translated into simplified game-readable shapes
- clean, warm color design without making every outfit neon
- no imitation of Roblox, Toca Boca, Disney/Pixar, or another recognizable franchise style

### Proportion target

Aim around a **stylized older-child read**, roughly 5–5.5 heads tall in full-body concept art. This is a direction, not a rigid anatomy formula. The key test is: they look like elementary/middle-grade kids, not toddlers and not teens/adults.

### Shared environment relationship

These kids should look like they plausibly arrived at the same neighborhood rec center after school or on a community-program afternoon.

They do **not** all need the same fashion lane.

# 3. Ari — playable detective

**Age:** 10  
**Gender presentation:** girl  
**State:** SPEC READY FOR ART

## Two-second read

A bold 10-year-old Black girl detective with moving braids, an oversized sporty jacket/cargo silhouette, and alert forward energy.

## Face + skin lock

- deep brown skin with warm/red undertone
- slim-soft oval child face
- almond-shaped dark eyes
- expressive slightly arched brows
- rounded nose tip with a distinct narrow-to-medium bridge
- natural full lips
- soft child cheeks that become more visible when smiling
- no makeup beyond natural lip/skin appearance

### Reject if

- face becomes round/toddler-like
- adult influencer makeup/lashes appear
- facial structure becomes interchangeable with Nia or Zuri

## Hair lock

- small-to-medium knotless-style braids / individual braids
- clean box-section logic visible in close art
- shoulder-to-upper-back length
- only a few selected clear/gold-toned beads near some ends
- braid grouping should feel weighted, not like hundreds of independent wires
- running motion: delayed swing after direction changes; bead ends lag slightly
- gameplay read: moving braid cluster + jacket silhouette

### Reject if

- braids emerge from an undifferentiated cap
- every braid has identical bead placement
- beads float/fuse
- braids move like loose straight hair

## Body + wardrobe

- average-tall for the group; slim/average child build
- kid-sized varsity/windbreaker layer
- graphic tee
- relaxed cargo jeans
- colorful high-top sneakers
- small studs
- detective icon as a charm/lanyard, not a giant magnifying glass prop

**Suggested color family:** deep teal/green layer + warm accent colors; do not make color her only identifier.

## Motion lock

- quick, bouncy run
- slight forward lean toward clues
- clue reaction: tiny shoulder pop / point
- fail: brief hands-on-knees disappointment, immediate rebound

# 9. Full-cast comparison requirements

Generate/review a single lineup showing all six in the same art style, neutral standing pose, similar lighting, and consistent camera distance.

### We must be able to answer YES:

- Ari and Nia are clearly different girls even if both have braided styles.
- Tre and Jayden are clearly different boys even if both have short textured hair/tapers.
- Malik does not read as a teenager/adult just because he is tallest.
- Zuri's puffs are structurally believable and not caricature spheres.
- Skin tones/undertones visibly vary without anyone looking gray/ashy/orange.
- No two faces appear to be one base with swapped hair.
- Clothing lanes vary: detective casual, school/varsity, basketball, artsy oversized, coordinated gamer/sport.
- All six still look like they belong to one neighborhood/game universe.

# 10. Shared AI concept-art base brief

Use this as the common visual foundation, then append the individual character block.

> Create an original fictional Black child character for a contemporary browser game, matching the specified age exactly. The character must look unmistakably child-aged, with natural child facial proportions, posture, hands, and body shape — not a miniature adult, teen influencer, toddler, or chibi baby. Use a warm polished 2.5D/dimensional cartoon style suitable for a modern kids game: expressive but not anime-exaggerated, slightly enlarged head/eyes only for readability, believable nose/lips/cheeks, clean shapes, soft dimensional shading, full-body game-design presentation. Preserve the exact skin depth/undertone, distinct facial anatomy, hairstyle construction/parting/length/volume/accessories, age-appropriate clothing silhouette, shoes, signature object, and movement personality in the character brief. Black hair must be structurally believable and follow its specific real-world construction; do not render hair as a generic cap, helmet, perfect sphere, or straight-hair mesh with texture painted on. Contemporary kid fashion, relaxed proportions, no adult makeup, glam posing, sexualized anatomy, or franchise imitation. Original design only.

### Shared negative guidance

> Reject adult/teen face or body, toddler/chibi proportions, heavy makeup, false-lash glamour, generic repeated face, impossible braid roots, floating beads, rigid ball-shaped puffs, straight-hair physics on textured styles, random hair accessories, fused fingers/hair/clothes, branded logos, copied franchise styling, and overly stereotyped "urban" presentation.

# 11. First visual test order

Do **not** generate 30 images of one character before checking the cast.

Recommended sequence:

1. **Ari + Tre together** — prove playable detective girl/boy readability.
2. **Nia + Zuri together** — prove two Black girls can be distinct beyond hair color/style.
3. **Malik + Jayden together** — prove two Black boys can be distinct beyond fade/taper vocabulary.
4. **All six lineup** — check age band, faces, undertones, silhouettes, cast chemistry.
5. Revise specs/prompts once.
6. Only then create side/back/motion-specific studies.
